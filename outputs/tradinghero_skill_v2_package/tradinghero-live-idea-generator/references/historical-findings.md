## Main Facts

- Strong discount / flash-sale mechanics are the clearest direct-conversion signal.
- In the full-history analysis, `强折扣秒杀` averaged about 9,741 GMV, versus about 2,967 GMV for non-strong-discount records.
- Top cases include:
  - 2025-06-18: `618秒杀`, 78,160 GMV, 30 flagship-year sales.
  - 2026-06-18: `2280元秒杀`, 63,840 GMV, 28 flagship-year sales.
  - 2025-08-07: `88折+抽1-4个月+五件套`, 41,738 GMV, 12 sales.
  - 2025-12-12: `主播礼品二选一`, 40,374 GMV, 15 sales.
  - 2026-03-19: `2391/2491/2591三轮秒杀`, 28,692 GMV, 12 sales.
  - 2026-01-22: `2490/2690/2890三轮秒杀`, 28,590 GMV, 11 sales.
- Gift play alone is not sufficient. It works best when attached to a clear price reason, scarcity reason, or conversion flow.
- Course/private-domain/pre-order flows are valuable for demand capture, but should not be judged only by same-day GMV.

## Effective Pattern Families

- Direct conversion:
  - Low price.
  - Flash sale.
  - Time limit.
  - Scarcity/limited quota.
  - Clear event title.
- Higher-touch conversion:
  - Course/private live class.
  - Homework/check-in.
  - 1 yuan pre-order.
  - Customer-service follow-up.
  - Return sale.
- Interactive enhancement:
  - Gift choice.
  - Blind box.
  - Lottery.
  - Egg cracking / scratch card / wheel style mechanics.
  - Use only after a price or conversion reason is clear.

## Known Risk Cases

- 2026-04-21: `2850/2950/3050三轮秒杀`, high UV, zero GMV.
- 2026-05-22: `3090/3140 + 礼品三抽一 + 加赠1个月`, high UV, zero GMV.
- Similar cases indicate that price, purchase reason, and sales follow-up may matter more than benefit complexity.
