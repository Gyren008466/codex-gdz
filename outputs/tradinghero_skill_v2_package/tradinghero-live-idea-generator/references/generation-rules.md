# Generation Rules

Generate exactly 3 plan options unless the user asks otherwise.

## Historical Mode

`参考历史`:

- Prioritize TradingHero Excel evidence and known effective patterns.
- Use PDF ideas only as inspiration.
- Prefer strong discount, flash sale, scarcity, and clear conversion reason when the goal is GMV.

`不参考历史`:

- Use broader live-commerce interaction mechanics.
- Label the logic as creative assumption rather than historical proof.
- Still obey gift-library and budget rules.

`混合模式`:

- Start with TradingHero evidence.
- Add one fresh mechanic or seasonal gift angle.
- Do not let the creative layer replace the price reason.

## Plan Types

Stable plan:

- Moderate execution risk.
- Avoid extreme low price unless the user requires it.
- Use a clear but simple benefit stack.
- Best for normal weekly livestreams.

Sprint plan:

- Built for GMV or sales breakthrough.
- Prefer strong discount, flash sale, low-price quota, time windows, and urgent framing.
- Best for month-end, big promotion, major market event, or user explicitly asking to push revenue.

Interactive plan:

- Built for live-room atmosphere and choice.
- Use lottery, blind box, gift choice, scratch-card, or staged benefits.
- Must still include a clear price anchor or purchase reason.

## Goal Routing

`冲GMV`:

- Prefer strong-discount flash sale.
- Prefer 2280-2691 yuan when allowed.
- Include scarcity or time pressure.
- Use gifts as add-ons, not the main reason.

`冲销量`:

- Can be more aggressive than GMV-focused plans.
- Estimate sales from base price and GMV range.
- Do not use monthly-membership cases as direct evidence.

`前置蓄水`:

- Prefer course, private live class, homework/check-in, 1-yuan pre-order, add customer service, or return-sale flow.
- Explain that same-day GMV is not the only metric.
- Include how the lead list converts in a later promotion.

`互动增强`:

- Use gift choice, blind box, egg cracking, scratch card, wheel, two-host gift stacking, or staged reveal.
- Require a price anchor.
- Warn if the plan is only gift complexity without a purchase reason.

## Price Rules

- 2280-2691 yuan: best for sprint, flash sale, month-end, major promotion.
- 2691-2890 yuan: good for theme, staged discount, extra months, limited-time benefits.
- 2890-3090 yuan: requires clear benefit boost or strong event reason.
- 3090-3250 yuan: high risk unless paired with course seeding, scarcity, private-domain follow-up, or high-value rights.
- Above 3250 yuan: not recommended as the main live-sale price unless the user explicitly avoids low-price promotion.

Discount formula:

`discount = base price / 3690`

Examples:

- 2280 yuan = about 61.8%.
- 2691 yuan = about 72.9%.
- 3140 yuan = about 85.1%.

## Benefit Budget Rules

0-50 yuan:

- Small coupon.
- Low-cost peripheral.
- Study material.
- Lottery qualification.

50-100 yuan:

- 50 or 100 yuan coupon.
- Small JD card.
- Small product-related gift.

100-200 yuan:

- Small electronics.
- Golden sticker/peripheral.
- Desk mat.
- Handbook.
- 15 days or 1 month extra membership.
- 100 yuan 京东E卡 when confirmed.
- 100 yuan 立减金 when price-affecting benefits are allowed.

200-300 yuan:

- Portable screen.
- Suitcase.
- Gold-themed gift.
- Extra months.
- Combined package.
- 200 yuan 京东卡 when confirmed.
- 200 yuan 立减金 when price-affecting benefits are allowed.

300+ yuan:

- Big-prize pool only.
- TV, projector, display, or other high-value gift should be scarce, not guaranteed for every buyer.

## Benefit Design Principles

- Benefits must support the purchase reason.
- Product-related benefits are preferred: extra months, indicator learning pack, order-flow material, handbook, desk mat.
- Physical gifts improve perceived value but should not replace discount logic.
- Keep 2-3 benefit layers at most.
- Make the user understand the deal within one sentence.
- 京东卡 is a voucher benefit and does not lower the TradingHero payment price.
- 立减金 is a price-discount benefit and must lower the final payment price.
- When using 立减金, state base price, discount amount, and final payment price.

## Historical Case Use

- Include at least 2 historical cases per plan.
- Use exact event names when possible.
- Mark whether the case is actual Excel evidence or PDF inspiration.
- Avoid presenting PDF-only ideas as proven.
