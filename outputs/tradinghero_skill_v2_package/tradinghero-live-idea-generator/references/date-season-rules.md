# Date And Season Rules

Use these rules when the user provides an activity date, month, holiday, promotion window, or market timing context.

## Month-Based Gift Direction

- January to February:
  - New year, Spring Festival,开工,财运寓意.
  - Prefer: 马年钥匙扣, 黄金手机贴, 黄金知识桌垫, 立减金, new-year themed wording.
- March:
  - Anniversary, spring learning, tool upgrade.
  - Prefer: 指标手册, 订单流桌垫, 黄金知识桌垫, course/private live class.
- May to June:
  - 618, summer practical gifts, direct conversion.
  - Prefer: 京东卡, 立减金, 0 yuan inventory bundle, flash-sale rhythm.
- September to October:
  - Learning restart,金九银十, desk/workflow upgrade.
  - Prefer: 指标手册, 订单流桌垫, study-pack framing.
- November to December:
  - Double 11, Double 12, year-end review, new year prep.
  - Prefer: 京东卡, 立减金, 台历 if confirmed, year-end review/next-year preparation wording.

## TradingHero Audience Fit

TradingHero users are more likely to respond to gifts that feel:

- Practical for trading study or desk setup.
- Directly connected to indicators, order flow, gold/futures knowledge.
- Cash-like and easy to understand.
- Time-limited when the goal is GMV.

Avoid making unrelated physical gifts the main purchase reason. Use them as extra value after the price reason is clear.

## Date Output Requirement

When using the date:

- Say which seasonal angle was used.
- Explain whether the gift is product-related, cash-like, festive, or interaction-oriented.
- Do not claim a seasonal fit if the date was not provided.
